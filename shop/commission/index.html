Print Commissions

Printmaking is a very labor-intensive process and I sadly never have enough time in the darkroom to make print editions of every photograph I make. If you would like to collect a print of an image that isn't already available in my shop, you have two options:

  1. Drop me an email to let me know that you'd be interested in a print (include the name of the image, or a link to it). I'll add you to a wait list and, in the future, if I get around to making an edition of that image, I will send you a link to where you can order prints. If you decide then that you do not wish to purchase a print, that's totally fine - this is simply a noncommittal way to helps me gauge interest in making print editions of certain images.

  2. If you know for certain that you'd like to own a print of an image, you can order a commissioned print. This commits me to producing a print for you using the same 12 week lead time as for my regular print editions. Your print will be mailed to you as soon as I have completed work; you will receive number 1 of 50 of a new print edition. I do not currently charge a premium for this service. To proceed, use the options below to place your order, then contact me to let me know which image you'd like to have printed.
